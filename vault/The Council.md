# The Council

Five seats. Five daily questions.

- **[[Elon Musk]]** — Speed: what am I waiting for that has no real reason?
- **[[Robert Greene]]** — Power: whose fear can I calm today? Who learned my name?
- **[[Jamie Dimon]]** — Detail: do I know my numbers cold, or roughly?
- **[[Charlie Munger]]** — Inversion: what guarantees failure — am I doing it?
- **[[Nassim Taleb]]** — Fragility: can one phone call ruin me today?

Where all five agree: **the ownership conversation IS the plan.**
