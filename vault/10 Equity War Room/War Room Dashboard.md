# ⚔️ Equity War Room

> **The equity conversation is not a favor you ask. It's a case you prove.**
> This room is where the case gets built — one receipt at a time.

## The battle plan
1. **[[Win Ledger]]** — every win, logged same-day, with numbers and a witness
2. **[[Value Scoreboard]]** — running totals: revenue added, costs cut, margin points gained
3. **[[Owner Intel]]** — what the owner actually cares about, fears, and will object to
4. **[[The Equity Pitch]]** — the pitch itself, built from the ledger, framed as a story

## The strategic logic (yours, written down)
- **Vision + operations is your edge.** You see the opportunity AND you can build the machine. Most owners have neither in one person. That combination is what equity retains — salary retains employees; equity retains *co-builders*.
- **The delegation gap is part of the case.** "Everything runs through me" sounds like a weakness — reframed, it's leverage: *the business's key-person risk is me*. Document it (Playbooks folder), then show the owner you're de-risking the company by systematizing yourself. That's what an owner pays equity for.
- **Frame in the owner's math, not yours.** Not "I deserve X%" — but "here's the enterprise value I've added, here's the plan to 10x, and here's why aligning my upside with yours is the cheapest way to get it."

## Timing triggers — raise the conversation after:
- [ ] A margin improvement lands and shows in the numbers
- [ ] A quarter where your fingerprints are on the growth
- [ ] You've documented 3+ playbooks (proof you build systems, not just heroics)
- [ ] You know the owner's #1 fear (from [[Owner Intel]]) and can answer it
