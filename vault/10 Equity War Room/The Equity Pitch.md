# 🗣️ The Equity Pitch

> Build it as a story — that's how you remember and how they'll remember.
> Draft ugly now. Polish only in the final week before the conversation (perfectionism containment).

## Structure
**Act 1 — Where the business was.** (before you: the numbers, the pain)
**Act 2 — What changed.** (3 best stories from the [[Win Ledger]], each with a number)
**Act 3 — The 10x future.** (the plan from [[Value Scoreboard]] — vivid, concrete)
**The ask.** Specific structure, specific milestones, aligned upside. Then *stop talking.*

## The ask — options to research
- [ ] Direct equity grant with vesting (cleanest, hardest to get)
- [ ] Options with performance triggers (easier yes)
- [ ] Phantom equity / profit share (owner keeps control — often the door-opener)
- [ ] Equity earned on milestones (e.g., X% at 2x EBITDA, more at 5x)

## Rehearsal checklist
- [ ] Told the pitch out loud to someone (you learn/lock things in by explaining — use it)
- [ ] Every objection in [[Owner Intel]] has a one-line answer
- [ ] The ask is a number, not a feeling
- [ ] I've decided my walk-away plan (BATNA) — equity talks change when you have one
