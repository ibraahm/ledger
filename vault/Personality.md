# Personality

**INTP** (16Personalities): pattern-spotter, systems mind, analysis paralysis,
starts under pressure, hyperfocus → burnout, bored by routine execution.

**Enneagram 3 — 99% match** (Truity): achievement- and image-driven,
adaptable, competitive. Superpowers: Ambition 20/20, Analysis 18/20.
Blind spots: Superiority 18/20, Image 17/20. Core fear: being exposed as
a failure. The treadmill is real — after a win I'm "already onto the next
goal," so wins must be *felt* on purpose.

**Design implications:** external deadlines announced to people · sprints
aimed at real events · delegation of routine · visibility used as fuel ·
scheduled recovery. See [[Comfort Zone Rules]].
