# Comfort Zone Rules

- **One uncomfortable act per day** — the call avoided, the ask delayed,
  the stranger unmet. Discomfort daily or the plan dies.
- **Announced deadlines only.** Tell someone the date the moment it's set.
- **Start ugly.** Open the file. Send the first sentence.
- **After every sprint, planned rest.** Burnout is not a badge.
- **Win, then feel the win.** One celebration per milestone, phone off.

*Mantra: I am the succession plan. I move today. Paper, not promises.
I am worthy even when I fail.*
